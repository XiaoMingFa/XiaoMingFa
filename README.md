### About Me

I'm a software engineer and quantitative systems architect based in Singapore with over 20 years of experience building deterministic, low-latency infrastructure at the intersection of **computer science, financial markets, and cryptographic protocols**.

After studying **Computer Science and Business & Finance at the National University of Singapore (NUS)**, I started my career in the early 2000s writing C++ FIX order-routing gateways, limit order book matching engines, and tick-data ingestion pipelines across APAC trading desks. Over the past decade, my work has centered on high-throughput Rust and Python systems—ranging from columnar analytical engines (Apache Arrow, Parquet, DuckDB) to on-chain DeFi execution, perpetual futures pricing, and decentralized settlement infrastructure.

I care deeply about **numerical precision, zero-copy memory layouts, predictable tail latency, and clean state-machine invariants**—because in both distributed systems and live order books, silent data corruption or an unhandled edge case costs real capital.
